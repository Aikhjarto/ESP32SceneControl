Digital outputs switch at the points of their sequence, every scene cycle. Times are in ms from the start of the cycle; before the first point an output keeps the state of the last one.

One scene cycle. Bars show each servo's delay, move, dwell and return, and when each output is on; the line is the current scene time.

While testing, the servo follows the slider at up to max_dps and ignores the scene. Release blends it back into the choreography.
